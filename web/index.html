About this map

Preliminary numbers. Roof counts, geometry and relative suitability are exact, but the absolute energy and capacity figures come from an uncalibrated single-day radiation pass and read roughly 2–4× high. A calibrated 12-day run will replace them; treat kWh/kW as indicative only for now.

Each tract is placed in a 2×2 quadrant by median-splitting per-household solar potential against energy burden. Priority tracts (outlined red) are high potential and high burden — where rooftop solar does the most equity good.

Roofs: Microsoft Building Footprints + USGS 3DEP LiDAR, shaded r.sun radiation, RANSAC roof planes, PVWatts yield. Tracts: US Census TIGER/ACS + DOE LEAD. Built with open-source Python; served static via MapLibre GL JS + PMTiles.
